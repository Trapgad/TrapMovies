About TRAP MOVIES

Welcome to TRAP MOVIES — your home for discovering movies, exploring new releases, and finding something worth watching.

TRAP MOVIES was created to make movie discovery simple, enjoyable, and accessible.

Our Story

TRAP MOVIES started when I was in SHS 2, with a simple idea and a growing passion for movies and technology.

At the time, I was still learning how to build websites and exploring what I could create with code. I wanted to build something of my own — a platform where people could discover movies, explore new titles, watch trailers, and learn more about the films they love.

What started as a small idea during my SHS days gradually became TRAP MOVIES.

As I continued learning and improving my skills, I kept developing the project, adding new ideas, improving the design, and turning the original concept into something bigger.

TRAP MOVIES is not just a movie website. It represents my journey as a young developer — from having an idea in SHS 2 to continuously learning, building, and bringing that idea to life.

And this is only the beginning.
